1Backdrop — a Mac app for exporting dribbble-ready shots

Assumptions: desktop-first frameless Mac window; the core loop is drop screenshot → pick template → tune background + frame → export. Three directions: 1a dark "tech" three-pane (closest to your reference), 1b the same app on the Fieldset design system (light, dense, indigo), 1c a full-bleed canvas with floating panels + batch/CLI flavor. The screenshot areas are live drop targets — drag your own UI shot onto any of them. Gradient + frame theme are tweakable (Tweaks panel).
